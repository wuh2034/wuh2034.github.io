VLM-Guided Interactive World Model

Active

🎓 Master's Thesis · TUM Visual Computing & AI Lab 📅 2025.12 – Present

  • World Models
  • VLM
  • SGLang
  • LoRA / PEFT
  • KV Cache
  • Data Engine

A chunk-level closed loop — the VLM decides, the world model generates, the result is fed back — that has distilled roughly 12,000 interaction trajectories (≈60k chunks, 12 hours of video) and fine-tunes LingBot-World v2 on them. Feed-forward Gaussian Splatting Reconstruction

Completed

🔬 Guided Research · TUM Visual Computing & AI Lab 📅 2025.01 – 2025.08

  • 3D Gaussian Splatting
  • VGGT
  • gsplat
  • Feed-forward Reconstruction
  • Novel View Synthesis
  • ScanNet++

Predicts 3D Gaussians directly from uncalibrated multi-view images — a frozen VGGT backbone plus a 32.65M-parameter DPT-style Gaussian head, no per-scene optimisation. About 1 s for 14 views, and 20.92 dB PSNR on ScanNet++ 2-view, beating Splatt3R by 0.58 dB and pixelSplat on all three metrics.

Large-Scale Metric 3D Reconstruction — Building TUM in a Day

Completed

👥 Semester Project · TUM Computer Vision Group 📅 2025.03 – 2025.08

  • 3D Reconstruction
  • VGGT-1B
  • MASt3R
  • NKSR
  • CityGaussian
  • Metric Scale

A scalable pipeline for building- and city-scale capture: ~1,300 video frames chunked into overlapping 40-frame batches, VGGT-1B per batch, MASt3R to recover metric scale across chunks, then global alignment into point clouds, meshes and Gaussians. Novel-view rendering reaches 26.37 dB PSNR outdoors and 21.88 dB indoors.
